The edge gives the price away.

The vein goes over the edge.

The vein runs through the stone.

The tell

Print stops where the cut begins.

Real stone is the same all the way through, so a vein carries over every edge and down the side. A printed porcelain face stops at the cut, and the edge shows a plain body.

Through-body porcelain is the exception, and on a kitchen floor it is often the better buy. It does not etch.

Save this for the stone yard.
