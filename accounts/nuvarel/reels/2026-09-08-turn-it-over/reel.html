The tell

Dense and grey or buff: stoneware. Chalky, pale or orange: earthenware.

The unglazed foot is the only place the clay itself shows. Stoneware is fired hot enough to seal itself. Earthenware is sealed only by its glaze, so a chip at the rim drinks.

For a vase, either. For what you eat from every day, stoneware.

Save this for the ceramics aisle.
