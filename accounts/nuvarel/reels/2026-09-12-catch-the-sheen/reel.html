Catch the light before you buy paint.

The tell

Real eggshell keeps a whisper of sheen.

Cheap paint marketed as "flat" hides brush strokes by going matte everywhere, so a raking light disappears into it with no highlight at all.

A quality finish, even one labelled matte or eggshell, keeps a thin, even sheen. Run a lamp low across the wall. That's the ribbon of light a cheap flat paint can't hold.

Turns out flat was never really flat.
